# CAM AI against the shop's programs: misconceptions and gaps

Phase 4, step 1, with the statuses of Phase 5 (your 11 decisions; validation at commit 0b9139e) and Phase 6 (moves between and into cuts, parallels, fewer coordinates; all 108 programs validated at commit 724cb91). CAM AI (`src/lib/cam/`, line numbers as of commit d9714ed) compared with the knowledge base (`docs/knowledge/README.md`,
rule ids in brackets) and, part by part, with the engineers' programs (`npm run cam-library`, which runs
CAM AI on each reference part's 3D model). Each gap: what the shop does, what CAM AI does (file:line at
the time of writing), the change, and how it is checked. Status: **planned**, **done** (with the commit),
**question** (for the shop), **kept** (CAM AI already does it the shop's way).

First comparison, one verified part (`Pa839d8d8`, a 304 x 150 x 20 mm plate): the engineers need 4
clampings, 1,364 lines, 16.2 minutes; CAM AI (Phase 5) 4 clampings, 10,283 lines, 24.1 minutes; Phase 6: 3 clampings, 1,979 lines, 13.5 minutes. In the simulation both
programs make the part (CAM AI's matches the model, nothing collides, every clamping sits where the program puts it). CAM AI on every reference program, next to the
engineers' one: `docs/knowledge/cam-validation.md`.

- **Better on two parts:** Pc18802fc had 2 clampings like the engineers, and no collision. P77d3db5b was 35 % faster.
- **About the same on two parts.**
- **Worse on two parts:** more clampings, on edges. On P1a0b9fb4 there were also two collisions and a match under 0.99.
- **Why, likely:** on the two worse parts the engineers finish in their last clamping on 36 and 20 mm parallels (a grip of 9 and 25 mm). The trial kept 40 mm there and needed edge clampings instead.
- **What is missing:** how much to grip in the later clampings. The package needs that rule before it can replace CAM AI's way.

**W1 to W4 tried again in Phase 5** (5607883, reverted in c4636ac). The same package with a rule for the later clampings: grip as deep as the outside still to cut allows, 1.5 mm under it. Against the same code without it, on 7 reference programs: better on 2 (P178b33e9 25.8 to 11.8 minutes, Pf08a2c99 85.2 to 81.1), worse on 4 (Pebfe7f9f and Pfae5765f from 2 to 4 clampings, on edges; P8e294283 54.7 to 96.1 minutes; Pa839d8d8 36.7 to 47.4), the same on 1; in all 264 minutes and 23 clampings against 223 and 19. Leaving the outside 1.5 mm above a 5 mm grip leaves more of it for the later clamping, whose grip then has to stay small and fails the vise check. W4 (no facing on an edge) follows by itself once the sides are cut to size from the main faces: at this commit 10 of CAM AI's 115 clampings on an edge still face (the engineers' none).

**Plans ranked with the operator's time** (676f61a). Of the plans that pass every check, CAM AI takes the one with the least machine minutes plus 2 minutes per clamping (turning the part over, parallels, stop). The 2 minutes are an assumption, not measured at the shop: question.
